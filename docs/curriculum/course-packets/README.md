These files turn parts of the academy roadmap into reviewable teaching packets. They are source plans for future implementation. They do not mean the courses, routes, labs, assessments, or credentials are live.

## Shared rules

Every packet follows these contracts:

- [No-assumed-knowledge standard](../NO_ASSUMED_KNOWLEDGE_STANDARD.md)
- [Reality versus Fiction curriculum](../REALITY_VS_FICTION_CURRICULUM.md) when a unit compares a popular claim with technical evidence
- [Local Models and LLM curriculum](../LOCAL_MODELS_LLM_CURRICULUM.md) for model concepts, local and hosted boundaries, learner-controlled work, source records, and review cadence
- [Lab, assessment, and credential standard](../LAB_ASSESSMENT_CREDENTIAL_STANDARD.md) for future activities outside the browser, completion evidence, and proposed credentials

No packet or shared clarity standard creates a course access gate, learner label, diagnosis field, or medicalized path. The platform does not ask a learner to disclose a diagnosis. Clear terms, purpose, context, inputs, steps, expected results, recovery, pauses, and alternate evidence routes are ordinary platform behavior for everyone.

## Publication boundary

A packet becomes a public course only after its content is represented in the canonical registry, receives bookmarkable open routes, passes source and freshness review, meets the relevant safety level, passes accessibility and beginner review, and completes separate staging and production evidence. Planning approval is not publication evidence.

Once published, course outlines, units, exercises, prepared evidence, lab instructions, assessment blueprints, rubrics, and source notes remain directly open. Optional preparation adds context and never unlocks teaching. Signing in may synchronize private progress, and credential requirements may control issuance only. Neither condition hides the learning material.
